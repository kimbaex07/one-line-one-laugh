# One Line, One Laugh

One original funny line, written for the "One Line, One Laugh" bounty.

> My password manager forgot my password, so now I am locked out by the one employee I hired to remember everything.

**Character count: 114** (gate: 1–140 characters, single line).

## Why it lands

The joke flips the job description: the one tool hired specifically to remember things is the one that forgot. "The one employee I hired" turns a piece of software into a disappointing staff member — you pay it in trust, and it repays you with a lockout. The twist lands in the second half: you don't just lose a password, you get locked out by your own memory's memory. It's a situation anyone with a dozen logins recognises instantly, with no setup to trim and no explanation needed.

MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=./out python3 main.py
